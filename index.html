Everything your money needs, nothing it doesn't

Built for real monthly budgets: salary, rent, cash, cards, cheques and the occasional dinner out.

Today

One number to check each morning

"Safe to spend today" is what's left this month divided by the days to go. A pace bar shows if you're ahead of the calendar.

  • Spent today, this month and saved, at a glance
  • "Needs your attention" for due bills and cheques
  • Alerts before you overspend
Budget

50/30/20, or your own plan

Your income is split into needs, wants and savings. Use the classic 50/30/20 or make your own plans for normal months, holidays or Ramadan, and switch in one tap.

  • Monthly limits per category, plus an overall limit
  • Daily allowance that adjusts as you spend
  • A salary is never counted twice
Accounts

Every account, and your net worth

Bank, cash, savings, credit cards and loans in one place. See what you own minus what you owe.

  • Transfers and card payoffs never count as spending
  • Fix any balance with "Adjust balance"
  • Import CSV statements from your bank
Plan ahead

See the next 13 weeks coming

The cash plan adds your recurring bills, salary and pending cheques to today's balance, so you spot the lowest point before it arrives.

  • Recurring items: weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly
  • Cheques and LCN, issued and received
  • 13-week or 12-month view

Private by design

Your finances are nobody's business. Smart Budget has no server, no ads and no analytics.

Stays on your phone

Everything is stored on your device and works without internet. No account to create.

Backup to your own Drive

Optional backup goes to a hidden folder in your Google Drive. The app can't see your other files.

Encrypted if you want

Add a passphrase and your backup is encrypted with AES-256 on your phone before upload.

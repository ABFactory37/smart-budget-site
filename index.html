Smart Budget

A private budgeting app for Android that tells you, every day, how much you can safely spend.

  • "Safe to spend today" and a pace bar for the month
  • Needs, wants and savings budgets: 50/30/20 by default, or your own plans
  • Bank, cash, savings, credit-card and loan accounts, with transfers and net worth
  • Recurring bills, cheques, a 13-week cash plan and CSV statement import
  • Statistics by category, month, year or any date range
  • Optional, encrypted backup to your own Google Drive

Smart Budget works fully offline. There are no ads, no analytics and no account to create. Your data stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive.
